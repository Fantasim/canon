## Known defect: dependent fields in multi-op edits (M4.1, first after M4)

Single-op edits and simple Undos are correct, and every M4 gate passes. A request that changes a
driver field and then its dependent values can still fail, or in some orders Undo wrongly
(API.md E1, E22). The cases:
- a list element or map value typed against the old branch;
- Add or Insert into a dependent list;
- nested type functions;
- a type through a ref;
- a record-parameter field;
- an Undo that restores a dependent before its driver.

Two attempts are kept on local branches, not pushed: `m41/ue22-wip` (Undo grouping, failed
review) and `m41/ue1-wip` (forward typing, unreviewed). The planned design: build the inverses
as now, verify each Undo by a dry apply, restore the smallest enclosing item whole where that
fails, and fix the forward typing in `applyset.go` `targetType`. Details, probes and root causes:
log-2026-09-29 "U-E22-r" and "M4.1 inputs".
